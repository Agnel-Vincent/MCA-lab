About woody Woodworks

A small workshop, three carpenters, and a rule about never using laminate.

woody started in 2016 in a rented shed behind a hardware shop in Ayyanthole, repairing furniture for neighbours who couldn't find anyone willing to fix rather than replace. Nine years later we're still doing that, alongside building furniture from scratch — same three people, same insistence on solid wood.

The name means "carpenter" in Malayalam. It felt more honest than anything we could have invented.

What we won't compromise on

  • Solid wood, always

    No laminate, no particleboard core. If it looks like wood, it is wood.

  • Joinery over hardware

    Mortise-and-tenon and dovetail joints wherever the design allows — screws hold things together for a decade, joints for a lifetime.

  • One workshop, no subcontracting

    Everything that leaves under our name was built by one of the three of us, in the same shed in Ayyanthole.

  • Sourcing we can explain

    We buy reclaimed and certified timber and can tell you where each piece came from, if you ask.
