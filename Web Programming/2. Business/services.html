Services

Every job starts with a conversation about how the piece will actually be used — everything below is a starting point, not a fixed menu.

  • Custom furniture

    Teak, rosewood, mango · 4–8 weeks

    Dining tables, beds, wardrobes, bookshelves and study desks, designed to your dimensions and built with mortise-and-tenon joinery rather than screws and brackets.

  • Restoration

    Any wood · 1–3 weeks

    Loose joints reglued, cracked veneer rebuilt, hardware replaced, and a fresh hand-rubbed finish. We keep as much of the original piece as we can.

  • Interior woodwork

    On-site fitting · project basis

    Fitted wardrobes, wall panelling, staircase railings and window frames, measured and installed at your home or office.

  • Design consultation

    At your site · 1 visit

    An hour walking through the space with you, talking material choice, joinery, and a realistic budget before anything is built.

  • Finishing & polishing

    Oil, wax or lacquer · 3–5 days

    For furniture built elsewhere that needs a proper wood finish — we match colour and sheen to whatever's already in the room.
