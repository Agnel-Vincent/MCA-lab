What we build

Four kinds of jobs come through the workshop most often. Full details are on the services page.

  • Custom furniture

    4–8 weeks

    Tables, beds, cabinets and shelving, designed around your room and built to last generations, not seasons.

  • Restoration

    1–3 weeks

    Bringing old or inherited furniture back — reglued joints, rebuilt veneer, new finish.

  • Interior woodwork

    Project basis

    Wardrobes, wall panelling, staircases and window frames, fitted on site.

  • Design consultation

    1 visit

    An hour at your site to talk through material, joinery, and what's actually possible with your budget.
